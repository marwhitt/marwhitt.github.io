Commercial trucking safety: Texas carriers against their peers

Which Texas-domiciled carriers show unusually high or low safety outcomes once fleet size and activity are accounted for? The project benchmarks carriers on FMCSA-defined measures, surfaces outliers inside fleet-size peer groups, and tracks how the benchmarks move over time. It is framed for a fleet operator who wants to know where they stand.

Source
Three FMCSA extracts joined on USDOT number: carrier census, crashes, roadside inspections
Snapshot
2026-07-27 · 6.5 GB of CSV
Measures
Crashes per 100M VMT · driver and vehicle out-of-service rate · violations per inspection
Scope
Texas carriers · crashes 2020–2024 · inspections 2023–2026

  • The inspection file is a rolling three-year window. Inspection measures use 2023–2026 and say so, instead of pretending to reach back to 2020.
